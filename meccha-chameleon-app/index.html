Is there a Meccha Chameleon app for iPhone?

Short answer: not an official one.

No. Meccha Chameleon is a PC game and there is no official iOS version of it. If the App Store shows you something using that name, it isn't from the people who made it.

What you can play on a phone is the same idea built for a camera: KAMO. It is a separate game, made by different people, and this page is on KAMO's own site — so read it as "here's the closest thing", not as a port.

What people are actually looking for

Nobody searches for a chameleon game because they want a chameleon. They want the loop: a thing is hidden in plain sight, somebody has to find it, and the moment it snaps into view is the payoff. That's why the clips travel — the reveal does the work, not the graphics.

KAMO takes that loop and puts it where you already are. You point your phone at anything in front of you — a desk, a rug, a brick wall — drop a small figure into the shot, and paint it with your finger until it takes on whatever is behind it. Then you send it and watch someone fail to find it.

The honest gap: a made-for-PC game has levels, art direction and progression, and KAMO has none of that. What KAMO has instead is that every round happens in your actual room, and the result is a real photo you can send to someone who doesn't have the app.

What a round looks like

Point at a surface with some texture — gravel, wood grain, a patterned rug. Place the figure against an existing edge so its outline is broken. Copy the colours immediately behind it onto the figure, patch by patch, until your own eye loses it. A score tells you how close you got. Then KAMO plays the reveal: the scene as everyone else sees it, then the wipe showing exactly where it was.

If you want the specifics — which surfaces swallow a figure, and the four mistakes that give one away every time — the hiding guide covers it.

Questions

Is Meccha Chameleon coming to iOS?

Nothing official has been announced. We're not the developer, so we're not the ones to ask — this page just answers the question people type into Google.

Is KAMO made by the Meccha Chameleon developers?

No. KAMO is made by Bliss Coach and has no connection to that game or its developer. We name it here because it's what people are searching for, and telling them the truth is more useful than pretending the query doesn't exist.

Is KAMO free?

Yes, free to download with no signup. KAMO+ is optional and unlocks every brush size, the full colour palette, more time per round, and exports without the watermark.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. KAMO is iPhone only, iOS 15.1 or later.

Do I need AR hardware or a recent phone?

No. It uses the ordinary camera. If your phone can take a photo, it can play.
