Games to play in a group chat

Most last two days. The survivors have three things in common.

The three properties that decide it

Watch a chat game die and it is almost always one of these:

  • Cheap turns. If taking part costs more than about a minute, participation collapses to the two most enthusiastic people.
  • No scheduling. The moment a game needs everyone present at once, it becomes an event, and events need organising.
  • Something to compare. A score, a time, a streak. Without it there is nothing to say afterwards, and a game with no aftermath does not come back tomorrow.

What passes

Daily puzzles pass all three, which is why score screenshots colonised every group chat on earth. Photo challenges pass — one picture, one question. Guess-the-place from a cropped map pass. Long-form trivia and anything needing a live lobby fail on the second rule.

A useful filter before you introduce anything: can the quietest person in the chat take a turn on a bus without telling anyone in advance? If not, it will not survive the week.

Hides as a chat format

A hide is a picture with a right answer, which makes it a photo challenge that can be lost or won. Making one takes about a minute; playing one takes seconds; and because it is a link, people in the chat who do not have the app can still play it in their browser — which is usually what kills these things.
