Hide-and-seek ideas for adults

The original breaks in an adult house. These variants do not.

Why the original stops working

Two reasons, and both are structural. Adults are large, so a normal flat has maybe four viable hiding places and they are exhausted in one round. And the seeker does all the work while everyone else crouches in a wardrobe having no fun — the standing-around-to-playing ratio is terrible.

Every good adult variant fixes one of those two problems: it either multiplies the hiding places, or it gives the hiders something to do.

Variants that fix the pacing

Ranked roughly by how little setup they need:

  • Sardines. One person hides, everyone else seeks, and when you find them you get in with them. The last to find the pile loses. It makes the crouching social.
  • Object hide. Hide a small object instead of a person. Suddenly the flat has two hundred hiding places instead of four.
  • Hot-and-cold on a timer. Object hide with the hider allowed to say only "warmer" or "colder", once every ten seconds.
  • Photo hide. Hide something, photograph it, and hand the photo over. The room stays intact and nobody has to be in it.

The photo variant, specifically

It is the one that works when people are sitting down, drinking, or not even in the same building. You hide an object in a scene, take the shot, and hand the picture to someone: the whole game fits inside one image.

KAMO is that variant, automated — you place a small figure in the camera view, paint it until it matches its surroundings, and send it as a link that opens in any browser. About a minute to make, twenty seconds to play.
