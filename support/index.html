Something broken, confusing, or missing? Email anthony@blisscoach.app and you'll get a reply from a person. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version makes it much faster to reproduce.

Common questions

The camera is black, or KAMO never asks for permission

KAMO needs camera access to work at all. Open iOS Settings › KAMO and turn Camera on. If you denied it the first time, that switch is the only way back — the app cannot ask twice.

Saving to my camera roll does nothing

Check iOS Settings › KAMO › Photos and allow adding photos. KAMO only ever writes the image you just made; it never reads your library.

The torch button does nothing

The light needs a device with a rear flash, and it only lights while the camera view is open. On models without one, the button doesn't appear.

I paid and KAMO+ isn't unlocked

Tap Restore on the paywall — purchases follow your Apple ID, so this also covers a new phone or a reinstall. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with the date of purchase.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so they're managed in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. The Lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase and never renews.

What happens to my photos

They stay on your phone. KAMO paints the camera frame on-device and never uploads it — the full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
