Ways people actually use a hide, beyond playing one round alone on your desk.

A hide is a link. That single fact is what makes most of these work: the person on the other end does not need the app, an account, or anything but a thumb.

  • Hide-and-seek for adults The childhood version does not scale. These variants do.
  • Group chat games What makes one stick: cheap turns, no schedule, and a visible score.
  • Bored with friends The real problem is not boredom, it is that nobody wants to decide.
  • Long-distance friends Time zones kill live games. Asynchronous ones are the only ones that last.
  • Party games with a phone Phones split a room by default. The good ones pass a single screen around.
  • Family gatherings Mixed ages, no rules to explain, and nobody has to leave their chair.
