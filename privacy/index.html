Privacy Policy — KAMO

Last updated August 13, 2026

This policy describes how Bliss Coach ("we") handles your information in KAMO and on this website. It applies to the KAMO iOS application.

What we collect

  • No account, no sign-in. KAMO has no registration and no login. We do not ask for your name, email, or any other identifier, and we hold no user profile for you.
  • Camera and photos: processed on your device. The camera frame you paint on, and the painting itself, stay on your phone. We never receive your camera feed or your photo library.
  • Hides you share: the finished image leaves your device. A challenge is a link that lets someone else try to find the figure you hid, in their browser, without installing anything — which means the finished image has to be hosted. When, and only when, you open the share sheet to send a hide, we upload that image along with the figure's position (needed to tell a correct guess from a wrong one), your round time and your coverage. Finishing a round, painting, or saving to your photo library uploads nothing. The image is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, in the United States. A challenge link carries a random 16-character identifier and is not password-protected, so treat sending one the way you would treat sending the photo itself.
  • The KAMO feed: a hide marked Public is shown to people you never sent it to. The share sheet carries a Public switch, which is on by default, and it decides this. With it on, your hide is listed in a feed inside the app, where anyone can play it, watch the reveal and react to it, next to the display name you chose — or a random one if you chose none. With it off, the hide stays reachable only through its own link. The switch is shown before you send, never after, and your answer is remembered for the hides that follow, so turning it off once is enough; a hide you never shared is never published either way. Every hide in the feed carries a Report control, you can block a creator so their hides stop reaching you, and content that is reported enough times is taken down automatically.
  • Everything expires. Hides and their images — public or not — are deleted automatically 30 days after they are created. We will delete one sooner on request: send us the link.
  • Usage and diagnostics: feature usage events, crash reports, and device information, used to fix bugs and improve the app.
  • Advertising and attribution: if you allow tracking when iOS asks, we and our attribution partners (AppsFlyer, TikTok, Meta) may use your device's advertising identifier to measure which ad or shared link led to your install, and how the app is used afterwards. If you decline, attribution falls back to Apple's privacy-preserving measurement and no advertising identifier is used.
  • Subscription status: managed by Apple and our subscription infrastructure (RevenueCat). We never see or store your payment card details.

How we use it

To run the app, fix crashes, understand which features people actually use, measure the effect of our advertising, and manage subscriptions. There is no AI model in KAMO. The only server-side handling of an image is storing and serving a hide you chose to share — so the person you sent it to can play it, and, if you left the Public switch on, so it can appear in the KAMO feed.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app: analytics (Amplitude), crash reporting (Sentry), attribution and advertising measurement (AppsFlyer, TikTok, Meta), subscription management (RevenueCat), and hosting for challenges (Supabase), under their respective data-processing terms. Of these, only Supabase ever holds an image, and only for a hide you chose to share. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention & deletion

There is no account, so there is no profile to delete. Photos you never share live only on your device, and deleting the app removes them. A hide you did share is deleted automatically — image included, whether it was public or not — 30 days after it was created, and we will delete one sooner on request: send us the link. You can turn off advertising tracking at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. To ask what diagnostic or attribution data is associated with your device, or to have it erased, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your data. Contact us to exercise them.
