What it is

KAMO is a hide-and-seek game played through your phone camera. You point it at anything in front of you — a brick wall, a rug, a cluttered desk — drop in a small figure called a kamo, and paint it with the colours of whatever is behind it until it disappears into the shot. A score tells you how well you blended.

Then it becomes a challenge: the person you send it to hunts for the kamo in their browser, with nothing to install, and an animated reveal wipes across to show exactly where it was.

What it is not

Worth stating because it gets miscategorised: KAMO is not an AR game — it uses the ordinary camera, with no room scanning and no LiDAR requirement, which is why it runs on old phones. It is not multiplayer in the lobby sense; there is nothing live to join, and a challenge is played whenever the other person opens it. And it is not affiliated with any other camouflage or chameleon game, on any platform.

The studio

Bliss Coach is a one-person iOS studio. Its other apps are AI coaching apps — bliss-coach.com — and KAMO is the odd one out: no AI, no coaching, just a camera and a figure that disappears.
