Camouflage apps for iPhone: what people actually mean

The phrase covers three unrelated things. Work out which one you want first.

The three things called a camouflage app

Search that phrase and you get an unhelpful mix, because three unrelated categories share the name:

  • Pattern generators — make a camo print for a wallpaper, a sticker or a design mockup. No camera involved.
  • Camo filters — overlay a camouflage texture onto a photo of you or an object. It is a skin, not a hiding place.
  • Camouflage games — you actually have to make something disappear into a real background, and you get judged on whether it worked.

If you wanted the game

The distinguishing feature of the third category is that there is a right answer. A filter always looks like a filter; a camouflage game only works if the thing genuinely stops being visible, which means you are matching real colours in real light with real shadows.

KAMO is that: you drop a small figure into the live camera view, paint it with the colours around it until you cannot find it yourself, and get a score for how well you blended. Then you send the shot to someone and watch them fail.

If you wanted one of the other two

Then KAMO is the wrong app and you should stop reading. It cannot generate a camo pattern, it will not apply a camo texture to a portrait, and it has no export for print. Those are pattern and filter tools, and there are plenty of them.

Questions

Does it work on any background?

It works best on anything with texture — brick, gravel, bark, a patterned rug. A flat evenly-lit wall is the hardest surface there is; see the surface guides.

Yes, free to download with no signup. KAMO+ is an optional upgrade.
